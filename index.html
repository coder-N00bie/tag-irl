TAGIRL

Tag, but the city is real. Run through real 360° street photos, grab power-ups, don't get caught.

or join one

How it works

  1. One player is the tagger. Everyone else is a runner and gets a head start.
  2. Click the arrows on the ground to walk (or W/↑ to go the way you're facing). Drag to look around; A D turn.
  3. Get within a few metres of a runner and hit TAG (or Space).
  4. Walk over items to grab them (3 slots) and use them with 1 2 3.
  5. Runners win if anyone is still free when the clock runs out.
  6. No friends online? The host can add bots to the room.
